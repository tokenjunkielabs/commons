Grants & Bids

Share:

South Coast AQMD has a number of programs that provide monetary incentives for implementing cleaner technologies.  These programs are generally rooted in the initiatives and policies adopted by the South Coast AQMD Governing Board or in state and federal programs designed to spread the use of cleaner technologies that reduce air pollution.  Incentive programs cover the gamut from large fleet replacement projects to the replacement of wood-burning fireplaces using gas-log inserts to new transportation systems that help reduce mobile emissions.

In addition, South Coast AQMD solicits bids for a full range of business services and supplies and technology development research projects, including the purchase of off-the-shelf equipment, development of new monitoring devices, contracted business and scientific services, such as landscape services or specialized computer programmers, and much more. 

This page lists active Program Announcement/Opportunity Notices (PAs or PONs) and Requests for Proposals (RFPs) and Requests for Quotation (RFQs).  For further information on available grant opportunities, please see the complete list of Funding Incentives.

Bidders' Conference:  Some PAs/PONs or RFPs/RFQs require attendance at a bidders' conference.  Please check the PA/PON or  RFP/RFQ to find out if the bidders' conference is mandatory.  Please confirm attendance by calling the contact person listed on the PA/PON or  RFP/RFQ.  BIDS WILL NOT BE ACCEPTED FROM ANYONE NOT ATTENDING THE MANDATORY BIDDERS’ CONFERENCE.

Please note that all PA/PON or RFP responses must contain completed certification forms.  You may request forms be mailed to you by telephoning the contact person listed for the PA/PON or RFP, or you may download the forms to your computer.  Generally, certification forms are included in the PA/PON or RFP, or may be downloaded here (PDF, 5.90 KB).

A Sample Contract document may be downloaded here.

If you have any questions or would like a copy of the PA/PON or RFP/RFQ mailed to you, please contact the person listed, either by telephone or e-mail.
