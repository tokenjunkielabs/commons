2055 Central Indiana Metropolitan Transportation Plan (MTP)

The Indianapolis Metropolitan Planning Organization (IMPO) will soon issue a Request for Proposals (RFP) for consultant support to develop the 2055 Metropolitan Transportation Plan (MTP). The MTP is the region’s long-range transportation plan and will guide transportation investments, priorities, and policy direction across Central Indiana over the coming decades. The selected consultant will support plan development through technical analysis, public and stakeholder engagement, project evaluation, and preparation of a final plan that meets federal requirements.

  • The optional pre-bid meeting has concluded.
  • Proposals are due October 6, 2026, at 5:00 p.m.
  • Interviews, if needed, are expected to occur November 4–5, 2026.

Government Relations at the Statehouse

The Indianapolis Metropolitan Planning Organization (IMPO) released a Request for Proposals (RFP) for Metropolitan Planning Organization for Government Relations at the Indiana State House. is seeking qualified legislative affairs support to represent and advance regional priorities during the 2027 Indiana General Assembly session. As the lead agency for coordinated regional planning across Central Indiana, IMPO works at the intersection of transportation, housing, economic development, land use, safety, sustainability, and overall quality-of-life concerns.

If interested, please review the RFP document and submit a proposal as outlined in the document.

  • Responses are due October 7, 2026 at 12:00pm.
  • Interviews, if needed, are expected to take place on October 23, 2026.
