Request for Proposals

Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) System, Grants Management System, and Implementation Services

The Ventura County Transportation Commission (VCTC) is requesting proposals from qualified vendors to provide a modern Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) system, a Grants Management System (GMS), and implementation services. The RFP, functional requirements, cost proposal form, and draft professional services agreement are available for download below. Questions and requests to attend the optional pre-proposal conference go to Lupe Acero, Finance Director, at lacero@goventura.org.

Key Dates

  • Released: September 14, 2026
  • Pre-proposal meeting: September 28, 2026, 10:00 AM (PT) — optional
  • Questions due: October 12, 2026, by 3:00 PM (PT)
  • Answers issued: October 26, 2026
  • Proposals / Bids due: November 9, 2026, by 3:00 PM (PT)
  • Anticipated award: March 2027 (anticipated Commission approval)

Meeting information

To attend the pre-proposal conference, email Lupe Acero, Finance Director, at lacero@goventura.org with your name and title, company name, email, and phone number.
